PointillViz imports an AnnData file (.h5ad) with coordinates in .obsm and expression in .X, .raw or a layer, or a SpatialData store (.zarr).

Open the file

  1. Press Upload on the home page.

  2. Choose the file.

    Browse

    Press Choose files and pick the .h5ad in your computer's file dialog; it is read in place, not copied. For a SpatialData store, press Choose a .zarr store.

    Path

    Press Path, paste the absolute path of the .h5ad or .zarr store and press Open. On a server without a screen, Browse is off: use Path.

To import several sections as one atlas, see Atlases.

Check the mapping

The Map step proposes an entry of your file for every tab of the viewer, in two views of the same mapping.

Tiles

Press the value at the foot of a tile, here predicted_cell_type, to choose another entry. The chips under the tiles add a field; the × in a tile's corner removes it.

Wires

One wire for each assignment, from your file to a tab. Drag from an entry's ring to a tab to add it; press a wire to take it out.

The ? of a field

Says what to choose, why PointillViz proposed its entry and how the viewer uses it.

Your file

Every entry of the file. Press one to see its values.

Home card

The title, organism, tissue and modality that the home page shows.

Before you convert

A wrong choice converts without an error. Space must hold the tissue coordinates, not a UMAP. Genes must name what the matrix holds: Raw counts, or a normalization such as log1p(CP10K). Cell types must be the cell annotation, not a cluster column.

Convert and open

  1. Press Convert.

    A tab marked not built was skipped; Log says why.

  2. Press Choose view, then Tissue or Quality control.

    The dataset stays on the home page.
