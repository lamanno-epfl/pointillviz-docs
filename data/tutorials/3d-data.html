Colouring, legends and plots work as for 2D data.

Orbit the volume

Drag to orbit.

Shift-drag or right-drag to pan.

Scroll to zoom.

Press F to fit the volume in view.

Gradient, the 90° rotation, gene Density and the Landscape tab are 2D only; images, cell outlines and transcripts are not drawn in 3D.

Cut the volume with Slab

Box

  1. Open Slab and keep Box.

  2. Drag the dot on a face of the box.

    The min and max sliders of X, Y and Z do the same.

Depth from Surface

  1. Press Depth from Surface.

    PointillViz estimates the tissue surface, drawn as a mesh, and each cell's depth below it.

  2. Lower max to keep the outer cells, or raise min to keep the inner ones.

    The ruler under the view has the same two handles.

A slab only hides cells: Stats still counts the whole volume. To analyse the cells inside, press Analyze active volume.

Select cells in 3D

Projection

  1. Press Lasso, then Projection.

    Pick Freehand Selection, Rectangular Selection or Circular Selection.

  2. Draw on the view.

    It selects every shown cell under the shape, at any depth.

3D Object

  1. Press Lasso, then 3D Object and 3D Volume.

  2. Press where the sphere should sit and drag outwards.

    The sphere keeps its place in the tissue when you orbit.

  3. Drag the centre dot to move the sphere and the outer dot to resize it.

    A coloured dot moves it along X, Y or Z only.

  4. Drag its surface to push it in or pull it out.

Compare two regions

  1. Make region A.

    Draw a shape or a sphere, or set a slab and press Analyze active volume; the region summary opens.

  2. Press Draw region B to compare at the bottom of the summary.

  3. Make region B the same way.

  4. Press Compare regions, or Compare A with shown cells for a slab.

    The window's tabs work as for 2D data.

Slice the volume into sections

  1. Open Sections and place the plane.

    Use current view cuts parallel to the screen; Azimuth, Elevation and Roll turn the plane.

  2. Set Count and Thickness, then press Generate sections.

    Only shown cells are cut, so an active slab limits the series.

  3. Click a section in the 3D Section Series window.

    With Sections open, the plane in the view marks it.

  4. Choose the Analysis Type.

    Categorical composition stacks the cell types of each section; Gene expression plots a gene picked in Genes.
