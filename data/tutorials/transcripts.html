Draw the molecules of chosen genes

  1. Open the Transcripts tab.

    It appears when the dataset holds a transcript table (see Add a transcript table).

  2. Type a gene into Search gene… and click its row.

    Up to 12 genes. A second click, or × on its chip, removes one.

  3. Zoom in to see every molecule.

    While a chip shows a ratio such as 1:28, one dot stands for 28 molecules.

  4. Keep them over other tabs.

    Leaving the tab hides the molecules; Transcripts in the toolbar shows them again.

Adjust the dots and the cells

Style

Crisp draws plain dots, which keeps close molecules apart better than the halos of Glow.

Min QV

Leaves out molecules below this quality value.

Cells

Dim greys the cells, also on other tabs, so no cell colour competes with the dots. Show keeps their colours. Hide leaves the molecules alone, over an image when one is on.

Show all transcripts

Adds every molecule as a grey haze, which tells an empty area from one without the gene.

Replay sequencing depth

  1. Pick genes and press Depth replay.

  2. Press ▶ or drag the slider.

    The depth runs from 1% to 100%.

  3. Read the charts.

    Stability near 1 means the gene's map is already there. A curve still rising at 100% means more depth would probably add more.

  4. Colour the cells by one gene.

    Choose it in Paint cells: each cell shows its count at this depth.

Note

The replay removes molecules at random from the data you have: it shows when a map stops changing, not what a deeper run would find.

Add a transcript table

  1. Press + beside Transcripts in the toolbar.

    For a 2D dataset with one section. At import, add the Transcripts field instead (see Importing).

  2. Enter the table's server path and press Inspect.

    One row per molecule, such as Xenium transcripts.parquet, MERSCOPE detected_transcripts.csv or CosMx *_tx_file.csv.gz.

  3. Check the columns and Scale.

    Scale multiplies the table's x and y into the cells' coordinates.

  4. Press Add transcripts.

    A dataset holds one table: a new one replaces it.

Caution

Use scale suggests a ratio of ranges, not of units. With molecules in µm and cells in pixels, enter 1 / pixel size, then check that an epithelial gene lies on the epithelium.
