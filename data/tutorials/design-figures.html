Lay out a figure

  1. Press Design in the export folder.

  2. Drag pictures onto the page, or press Add all.

    A view brings its letter, colour key and scale bar; drag any of them.

  3. Set the page in Page.

    Its size, from one journal column to a slide, and the files to write: PNG, PDF or SVG.

  4. Zoom into a region: select a picture and press I.

    Drag the box over the part to enlarge.

  5. Add text and arrows from the toolbar.

  6. Press Save.

    The files go to the exports folder, and the figure joins the folder; Edit design reopens it.

A picture holds the pixels it was taken with. For a sharp close-up, zoom in the viewer and press C there.
