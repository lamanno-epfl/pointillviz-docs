Find a pair

  1. Open the Cell Comm. tab.

    It appears when the dataset has cell types, and lists the LIANA+ consensus pairs the gene panel measures.

  2. Type a gene into Ligand or Receptor.

    A prefix or a complex subunit also matches.

  3. Choose a Sort.

    Default ranks the most cell-type-specific pairs first and Low expression pairs last.

Each pair names the sender → receiver cell types that express it most specifically. Low expression: no cell type has 5% of its cells expressing the ligand, or the receptor.

Score a pair

  1. Click a pair.

    In an atlas, pairs are listed but not scored.

  2. Choose Co-variation or Proximity.

  3. Press Show map.

    Hover a cell to read its value.

Co-variation

R is positive where a cell’s receptor and its neighbours’ ligand are high or low together, negative where they oppose. z and p test it against no spatial relation, uncorrected for the number of pairs. The map shows each cell’s share of R.

Proximity

The readout is the share of receptor-positive cells with a ligand-positive cell within the Within distance (drag the slider or the curve), and their median distance to the nearest one. A cell counts as positive above Min. expression. The map colours each reached cell by that distance.

Change the settings

Co-variation takes the settings of LIANA+; the ? beside each says what it does.

Neighbours

The k nearest cells, or a Kernel whose weights fall with distance: gaussian, exponential, linear or misty_rbf.

Map

Each cell’s share of R, or a local score of LIANA+: local Moran’s R, cosine, Pearson, Spearman, masked Spearman, Jaccard, product or normalised product.

Hide low–low, Categories

Hide low–low sets every cell that is not high–high to zero and makes p one-sided. Categories colours each cell by its ligand and receptor around it: both high, both low, or one of each.

P-value, Min. non-zero

p is Analytic or comes from 100 or 1,000 permutations. A pair whose ligand or receptor is non-zero in fewer cells than Min. non-zero is not scored.

Apob → Lrp2 under four settings:

Kernel

  1. Neighbours
  2. Kernel
  3. gaussian

Weights fall with distance: about 27 neighbours at 18 µm, and R goes from 0.63 to 0.54.

Cosine

  1. Map
  2. Cosine

From 0 to 1, whatever the level: a thin layer beside the yolk sac lights up too.

Product

  1. Map
  2. Product

Grows with both genes: only the yolk sac stands out.

Categories

  1. Categories

High–high cells follow the yolk sac; most of the tissue is low–low.

Find the senders and receivers

The heat map splits the score by cell type: rows are senders, columns receivers, and a square is the part from those two types.

Senders and receivers

Picks the cell-type level, when there are several.

Top 8, Top 16, All

Shows the cell types with the largest values.

Hover

Gives a square’s value and full cell-type names.

Senders and receivers are where the ligand and the receptor are expressed; a score does not show that a signal passes.
