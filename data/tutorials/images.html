The pictures show the mouse colon (Xenium 5K) and its two images.

Show an image

  1. Press Image in the toolbar.

    It is greyed out when the dataset has no image.

  2. Pick H&E or Xenium DAPI.

  3. Choose Adjust channels and levels…

    In the tab, the eye buttons stack both images; lower the DAPI’s Opacity to see the H&E through it.

Images are drawn in the 2D spatial view only, not in the embedding view or the Expression Landscape.

Dim or hide the cells

In the Image tab, Cells switches between Show, Dim and Hide.

Dim

The tissue shows through the cells; cell outlines stay sharp. The tab opens on Dim.

Hide

The image alone. A hover still names the cell under the pointer.

Place a cell type in the tissue

  1. Open Types and set Color by to Lineage.

  2. Press None, then tick the types to place.

    Here Goblet cells and Mural cells; the other cells turn grey.

  3. Read the tissue under them.

    Goblet cells (yellow) sit on the mucus of the crypts, mural cells (green) in the muscle layer.

Adjust the image

H&E

Levels

The left and right handles set the black and white points, the diamond the gamma; the boxes take typed values. Double-click the diamond for a gamma of 1.

Saturation

0 shows the scan in grey, 2 doubles its colour.

Reset

The circular arrow restores the levels and the saturation.

Fluorescence

Colour

Click the dot before a channel’s name and pick a colour.

Levels and Auto

Levels work as for the H&E, in the image’s own units. The wand fits them to what is on screen; the circular arrow restores the file’s levels.

Channels

An image with several channels lists them: click a row to show or hide it, Alt-click to show it alone. Up to 8 show at once.

Add an image

  1. Press + beside Image.

    It is off for 3D datasets and atlases; the ? beside it says why.

  2. Type the server path of the image and press Inspect.

    TIFF, OME-TIFF, QPTIFF, OME-Zarr, PNG and JPEG are read.

  3. Check Scale and Offset.

    Scale is the cell coordinates per image pixel; Offset places the image’s top-left corner.

  4. Press Add image.

    It appears without a reload.

An added image cannot be moved. Check it with the cells dimmed and their outlines on: each nucleus should sit inside its cell.

Images inside an .h5ad or a SpatialData store are picked during the import: see Importing.
