The pictures follow the mouse tissue array (Xenium 5K): one .h5ad that holds 14 tissue sections.

Import sections as one atlas

  1. Press Upload, then Folder.

  2. Open the sections.

    One file

    Press the file's row in its folder. Its sections are the values of one .obs column.

    A folder of files

    Open the folder and press no row. Each .h5ad in it becomes one section.

    SpatialData stores

    A .zarr store counts as a file: split one by a column, or open a folder of them. Table and Coordinate system appear when the stores offer a choice.

  3. Press Scan.

  4. For one file, set Sections from.

    For the tissue array, choose tissue.

  5. Choose the Cell types column.

    The tissue array has none: choose None.

  6. Tick the Groups.

    Columns with one value per section, such as a condition, become groups to compare.

  7. Press Import atlas, then Open atlas.

Choose the sections to show

Click a tile

Shows only that section. Ctrl/⌘-click adds or removes one and Shift-click a range; with Multi on, every click adds or removes.

[]

All, Single, [ and ]

All shows every section, Single only the first chosen; [ and ] step to the previous or next section.

Group

Orders and colours the tiles by a group; press a group's name to show all its sections.

Detail

Draws a random 50% to 5% of each section's cells for a lighter view; Full draws them all.

Compare groups in the Atlas tab

The tissue array has no cell types and no groups, so its Atlas tab shows the overview only.

Statistics over

Takes every section of the atlas or only those shown, and counts all their cells at any Detail.

Composition

One bar of cell-type shares per section; Group rows by pools the sections of each group. Click a row to show only that section.

Differential abundance

Choose a Covariate, Group A and Group B: a volcano plot and a table show each cell type's log2 A/B and p adj.

Each section counts as one replicate: several sections from one animal are not independent and make p too small.

Work in the other tabs

The sections shown

Every other tab works on the sections shown, as one dataset with one colour scale.

Every cell, or the cells drawn

Analyses run on the server, such as differential expression, read every cell at any Detail; plots counted in the browser count the cells drawn, and below Full they say so (“25% sample”).

One section at a time

Embed and the UMAP inset appear only while one section is shown.

Not in an atlas

Images, cell outlines and transcripts: an atlas holds cells, genes and annotations.
