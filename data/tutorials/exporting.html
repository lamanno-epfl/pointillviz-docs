Save a picture or a figure

  1. Keep each view: press C.

    Kept views last until the page is reloaded, also when another dataset opens.

  2. Press S.

    The Export dialog opens with the kept views ticked.

  3. Tick the views for the figure.

    Ticked views become panels a, b, c: drag one to move it, type under it to retitle it.

  4. Set out the panels.

    Columns, Spacing, Labels and Titles appear once two views are ticked.

  5. Choose the format and the resolution.

    The view on screen is drawn again at up to 8×; a kept view cannot be enlarged, so Max is the largest size that enlarges none.

  6. Name the file and press Save figure.

    With one view ticked, the button reads Save.

Shift+S saves the view on screen at once, as a PNG at 1× without legend or scale bar.

Export data

  1. Press Export data, the download button.

    Selected lists what the download holds: by default, the dataset's columns and every result computed so far.

  2. Press Export.

    The browser saves one ZIP.

Choose what goes in

Press Adjust in the panel to open the Export builder. The tabs stay usable: run an analysis now, and its result joins the builder.

My Download

The ZIP as it will arrive. Click a file to see its first rows; the pencil renames it, or the ZIP. Under Header preview, drag a column to move it, rename it, or press × to leave it out.

Add Content

The same content by subject: tick what to include. A result is listed once the session has it: draw a lasso, and Region membership and Region geometry appear. Until then, its row says what to run.

In Pathways, Open designer lays out a report page that repeats for every chosen pathway. Create report saves it as a PDF and adds it to the download.

Apply keeps your choices for Export. Discard, or Esc, returns to those you had before Adjust.

Read the files

FileHolds
cells.csvOne row per cell, in the dataset's order: row k is cell_index k. Per-cell results are columns: a lasso adds region, A for the cells inside.
tables/Results whose rows are not cells: cell-type distribution, niche enrichment, colocalization, differential expression, distance-gradient profile.
regions/What you drew or chose: outlines, slab bounds, category names and colours.
figures/The pathway report.
manifest.json, README.txtWhere the export comes from, and how to read it.

The download holds no gene expression per cell: it carries annotations, coordinates and results.
