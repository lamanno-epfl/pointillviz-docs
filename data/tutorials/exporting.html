PointillViz exports in two separate ways. A folder collects data and results while exploring and writes them as one AnnData file with its pictures. A figure sets views out as the panels of one picture, as PNG, PDF or SVG. They share one thing: pressing C keeps a view for both.

The dataset stays as it is

Neither way changes the dataset or the original .h5ad. A folder export writes a new file, cut from the original file to the chosen cells, with the folder's columns added.

Export a folder

For data and results: selections, plots, tables and views go into a folder, and one .h5ad is written, with the pictures beside it. Read this to hand cells, columns and numbers to a script, a collaborator or another PointillViz. A folder belongs to one dataset. Everything in it is kept as it was when added: the numbers and the picture are stored with the item, and nothing is calculated again when the file is written.

The idea, in four steps.

1 · Keep a moment

Press C for the view on screen, or the + beside a lasso or a plot. It is like a screenshot, but one that also keeps the settings behind it. Each item gets the time it was taken, and the number on the folder button grows with every item.

2 · Go back to it

Open an item, and PointillViz shows it again exactly as it was. Change it, then either replace the old item or keep the change as a new one. What was on screen before is not touched: when the item is closed, PointillViz returns to it. So a view, a lasso or the settings of a plot can be kept as an argument, as it was at that moment.

3 · See where it lands

The folder shows the AnnData file before it is written: every column of obs, which can be kept or left out, and a green line from each item to the columns, tables and pictures it adds.

4 · Take the results with you

The export writes one .h5ad with the results already calculated: a lasso becomes a column with 1 for the cells inside and 0 for the rest, a plot keeps its numbers and its picture. Nothing is calculated again. The file can be opened in Python, or in PointillViz as a new dataset, for example with only the cells of one lasso.

Still growing

The Export is new. The idea above stays, but some parts may still change; this page follows the current design, in which the Export fills the window and an item is edited under a bar across the top.

Keep a moment

Each dataset starts with one folder, named after the dataset and the date. The folder button in the toolbar shows its name and, once it holds something, how many items. Three controls put things in it or open it.

The + button

Export

Appears beside every plot, result and selection that can be kept; it is grey while there is nothing to keep yet.

Add an item

Add. Press the + (below, the one beside the cell count). The item goes into the active folder and the Export opens over the whole window with the new item selected, its title ready to type. Press Enter to keep the title, then Back to the viewer or Esc to continue exploring.

Remove. A + that has been pressed shows a tick. Pressing it again takes the item out of the folder; Undo in the message brings it back.

Press the folder button afterwards, and the folder opens with the new item in it, the lasso as its picture:

A selection or a plot

A + beside a lasso, slab, 3D volume, lens, gate, community or legend keeps a selection: the set of cells and, for a lasso, its outline. Opened again later, the lasso lies on the tissue as drawn and the region panel shows its composition and top genes again, with Draw region B to compare available. A + beside a plot keeps the numbers it draws, a picture, and the settings of the moment, so the plot can be opened again later.

What the + keeps

Export

Pick a kind of item to see how it is stored in the file. The pictures come from the AnnData file view: the item's card, and below it each place in the file it writes to.

A lasso or another region

A column of 1 and 0, and the outline

In obs the lasso becomes the column pv_in_lasso: 1 for every cell inside, 0 for every cell outside, here 253,802 inside. The outline as it was drawn is kept as pv_region_geometry in uns, next to two tables of the region panel: its cell-type composition and its top genes. When the selections of a folder do not overlap, pv_region also names the selection of each cell. A slab, a 3D volume and the lens are stored the same way.

The outline also brings the lasso back: opened again, it lies on the tissue as it was drawn.

The cell types that are shown

A column, and which types were shown

With some types hidden in a legend, the + keeps the cells of the shown types as the column pv_in_cell_types (1 shown, 0 hidden; here the four liver types, 76,931 cells) and the list of shown types as pv_shown_types. Communities and spatial-pattern classes are stored the same way.

A gate

The gated cells, and the gate itself

The + for the cells writes the column pv_in_gate, 1 inside the gate and 0 outside. The + for the plot writes the counts per type (pv_gate_counts), the gate's shape (pv_gate) and its picture.

A painted gene

The values of every cell

The gene view keeps the values that paint the tissue as a column, here pv_expr_apoa1 (0 to 152), and the view as a picture.

A plot

The numbers and the picture

A plot keeps the numbers it draws as one table in uns and its picture under figures/, numbered in folder order. The table holds what the plot shows, one row per group or per dot:

Violin: pv_violin, 20 rows. Marker dot plot: pv_marker_dotplot, 380 rows. Cell-type distribution: pv_cell_type_distribution, 190 rows. Ridgeline: pv_ridgeline, 25 rows. Cell-type hierarchy: pv_types_hierarchy, 213 rows. The same holds for colocalization, niche enrichment, the alluvial diagram and the landscape view.

A ligand–receptor pair

A score per cell and two tables

The pair keeps a value per cell as a column (pv_lr_gzma_f2rl2_morans), the matrix of senders and receivers (pv_lr_matrix, 3,600 rows), the ranked pairs (pv_lr_pairs, 200 rows) and its picture. A pathway card of the gallery keeps the score of its pathway the same way.

What lies under the lens

Three tables and a picture

Under the lens keeps the composition of the cells in view (pv_lens_composition), the histogram of the painted value (pv_lens_paint_histogram), where the lens stood (pv_lens_snapshot) and a picture.

A distance gradient

The profile and the line

The gradient keeps its profile along the line (pv_gradient_transect, one row per bin), the line itself (pv_gradient_geometry) and a picture.

A comparison of two regions

The comparison table

The comparison window keeps its table, here the cell types of region A against region B (pv_compare_celltypes), and its picture; the volcano plot is kept the same way. Region A and region B each have their own + that keeps their cells as a column.

A result of a tool

Whatever the tool returns

A tool from the Tool Store keeps each result it returns: a value per cell becomes a column, here pv_log10_local_density from Local Cell Density, a table goes to uns, a picture to figures/.

Go back to a moment

Every item in the folder is a moment you can return to. Opening one is a visit: the viewer shows the item exactly as it was, you can change it, and when you leave, the viewer goes back to what you were working on.

Moments over time

Keep the same specimen in different states

Each press of C keeps the view as it is at that moment: its orientation, its zoom and its colouring. Here the same 3D embryo was kept four times, turned and painted differently each time.

In the folder they are listed in order, each with the time it was taken:

Open and come back

Select an item and press Open in the viewer. The viewer turns to the item's orientation and colouring, and a bar over the toolbar says Editing with the item's title. What you were looking at is not lost: leaving the item brings it back.

Three ways to end the visit

Save changes makes the item what is on screen now. Save as new leaves the item as it was and adds what is on screen as a new item. Discard (or Esc) leaves everything as it was. Afterwards the Export opens again at the same item.

What you can change

A selection

The tissue and the region panel stay usable; the lasso lies on the tissue as it was drawn, and the region panel analyses its cells again. Draw a new lasso in the bar replaces the outline.

A plot

Only the plot's own panel stays usable, where it lives; tabs, toolbar and tissue are greyed out. Change its controls, then save.

A view

The tissue and the tabs stay usable: turn, zoom, paint another gene, then save.

What cannot be opened

Figures and documents, and items marked frozen. They can still be exported.

Compare two selections

Set two kept selections against each other

Select a kept selection and press Compare with…. It becomes region A; the menu offers every other kept selection, and the cells selected on screen, as region B. The region comparison then opens with both, without changing either item.

With only one selection in the folder the menu says so; keep a second lasso first.

Look into the folder and the file

The folder button, or S, opens the Export. It shows the same folder in two ways: as a list of the moments kept, and as the AnnData file it will become. The Export is a mode of its own: it fills the window, with a green Export badge, the name of the dataset and the button Back to the viewer in its header. Two views switch in the header: Items, what was collected, and AnnData file, the file as it will be written. The folders stay on the left in both.

Items

Export

Always available.

The list

Select. Click a row; the right side shows its picture, its kind and time, how many cells, and under Goes to where it lands in the file.

Rename. Click the title, or press Enter on a row, type, and press Enter.

Reorder. Drag a row, or press Alt+↑ or ↓. The order numbers the files in figures/.

Remove. Press Delete on a row, or Remove; Undo brings it back.

The item menu and the buttons

The three dots on a row open Rename, Open, Duplicate, Move to and Remove. The same actions stand as buttons under the picture; Open in the viewer is the green one. A selection also has Compare with….

Flags

new marks an item added after the folder was last written. stale marks an item whose result has changed on screen since it was kept; the kept version stays as it was. frozen marks an item that cannot be reopened.

A figure from several views

Click one view, then Cmd/Ctrl-click more; the right side offers Figure of n. The views become the panels of one picture, with one legend, as a new item. The figure dialog (choose A figure above) offers more choices.

AnnData file

Export

Always available; the Columns chip opens it too.

Lines from each item to its place

On the left every item is a card, on the right the file is a tree: obs with its columns, then obsm, var, uns, X and figures/. A green line runs from each card to every column, table or picture it produces. Moving the mouse over a card or a row lights its lines.

Tick, order, rename

Include or leave out. Untick a column. Reorder. Drag a row or use ▲ ▼. Rename. Press the pencil of a column the folder adds; its name starts with pv_. Columns of the dataset keep their names. The arrow of a row shows its first values. The same ticks work for var and obsm. A name used twice turns red and blocks the export.

Edit as table opens the same columns as a table with the first rows, with Apply and Discard.

Cells

Export

Always available.

Choose the rows of the file

The row The file above the list holds the Cells chip. All cells writes every cell. Visible now writes the cells shown at that moment and freezes them; the file gets a column pv_opacity. A selection in this folder writes only its cells.

Folders

Export

Always available.

Choose, name and delete

The last folder clicked is the active one: it receives everything added with a +. Click the name above the list, or press F2 on a folder, to rename it. New folder at the bottom, or Move to → New folder… in an item's menu, makes another; an item can also be dragged onto a folder.

The … beside a folder opens Rename, New folder, Export and open as new dataset…, Download CSV bundle and Delete folder. Undo brings a deleted folder back; deleting the last folder creates a new one.

Files written

After an export, the files written so far are listed under the folders. Click one to browse it in a tab of its own in the header.

Save as and the expression matrix

Export

Always available.

Format

Save as offers AnnData (.h5ad). SpatialData is listed as coming soon and cannot be chosen.

The expression matrix

The switch Expression matrix in the footer decides whether the matrix and the layers are written. Without it the file holds annotations and results only and is much smaller. The footer says how many items, which cells, and whether the matrix comes along.

Generate

ExportDocument

Always available. Pathway report needs pathway scores that have been calculated.

Make something from the session

Generate, at the right of the folder name, opens Pathway report… (the finished PDF is added to the folder as a document), Edit obs table… and Figure from kept views….

Write the file

When the folder holds what you need, write it as one file. The footer of the Export shows what will be written and holds the two ways out.

Download .h5ad

One file, written on the server, downloaded by the browser

Press Download .h5ad. The header counts the progress; Cancel stops it. When it is done the header names the file, its size and how many cells it holds, and the browser downloads it as a ZIP.

A message then asks whether to keep the folder; nothing needs to be pressed to keep it.

Open in PointillViz

Continue with the result instead of downloading it

Press Open in PointillViz and give the new dataset a name. The same file is written and converted into a dataset on this machine; Open it in the message opens it. With Cells set to a selection, this turns a lasso into a dataset of its own.

What is in the ZIP

<dataset>_<folder>.h5ad      the chosen cells, the folder's columns in obs, its tables in uns
figures/NN_<title>.png    every view, plot and document, numbered in folder order
manifest.json                what the file holds and where it came from
pointillviz_schema.json      the field mapping, to open the .h5ad in PointillViz again
README.txt                   a short list of the files and of every item

Built from the original file

The cells are cut from the original .h5ad, so the expression matrix, var, the layers and the original uns come along. If that file is no longer where it was, the converted data is used instead, which has the matrix and every column the viewer shows but no var annotations; the manifest says which. A name that already exists gets a number; nothing is overwritten.

Files written

Browse a written file without leaving PointillViz

Every file written so far is listed under Files written, below the folders. Click one: it opens as a tab of its own in the header, with its columns, matrices and tables.

CSV bundle

Plain tables for a spreadsheet

In the folder's menu, Download CSV bundle saves the session's results as CSV and JSON files, with the folder's column choice applied. It is a plain download, not the AnnData file.

Keep a view

ExportView

Always available.

Press C

Press C, or Capture view in the camera menu. The view on screen is kept as pixels, exactly as it looks, with its legend and scale bar. Choose the panels

Export

Appears in the figure dialog.

The tiles on the left

Tick. Each kept view is a tile; ticked tiles become panels, lettered in order. All and None tick or untick every tile.

Rename. Click the title under a tile and type. Reorder. Drag a tile. One alone. Alt-click a tile to export it alone.

Discard. The × on a tile, or Delete, discards it; Clear discards all; Undo brings them back.

The view on screen. The tile marked On screen is the live view; Keep keeps it too.

Layout

Export

Appears when two or more views are ticked.

Set out the panels

Columns. The − and + set the number of columns; the grid, such as 2 × 1, is written beside them. Spacing. Tight, Normal or Wide. Labels. a b c, A B C, 1 2 3 or none. Titles. Each view's title above its panel.

The preview above the rows shows exactly what will be saved, and the line under it gives the size in pixels.

Format and resolution

Export

Always available in the dialog.

Choose the file and its size

Format. PNG, PDF or SVG. Resolution. 1×, 2×, 4× or 8× of the screen size for the view on screen, which is drawn again. Kept views are pixels and cannot be enlarged: the choices that would enlarge them are left out, and Max gives the largest size that enlarges none, here 4.55×.

Legend and scale bar

Export

Always available in the dialog.

What is drawn beside the panels

Legend. Show adds the legends of the views in one rail beside the panels, a cell-type legend and a gene colour bar together; Small, Medium or Large sets its size. Scale bar. Show draws one in each panel; for a single view its length can be typed, and Auto restores the calculated length. With several views each bar is sized per panel.

In the preview, the legend rail stands at the right of the panels with the letter of the panel it belongs to.

Save

Export

Always available in the dialog.

Name it and save

Filename. Type a name; the default follows the dataset. Save. Save figure writes the file and downloads it; with a single view the button reads Save. Cancel or Esc closes the dialog; the kept views stay until the page is reloaded.

Export

Always available.

Shift+S saves the view on screen as a PNG at 1×, without legend or scale bar and without a dialog: the tissue as it is on screen, on its background.

A figure from the sheet
